An Apple Watch app that plays a sound when you move your arm.

## Contact

- GitHub Issues: https://github.com/888man/armsound-support/issues

Please send bugs, requests and questions there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an Apple Watch?

**Yes.** Recording and judging happen on the Apple Watch. The iPhone is where you
name a motion and pick its sound.

### Nothing plays

Check these in order.

1. Did you press Start on the watch? Judging only runs after you start
2. Is a group selected? Only one group can be active at a time
3. Is the volume set to zero? It is per motion
4. Is the device in silent mode?

### It does not fire where I expect

It will not be perfect at first. **You fix it when it is wrong.**

- It should have fired but did not → add that motion with Learn
- It fired when it should not have → hold it back with Ignore

Every correction moves it closer to the way you actually move.

### How many motions of my own can I keep?

One for free. You can re-record over it as often as you like. With the purchase
you can keep as many as you like.

### I bought it but the features are still locked

Open **All features** and tap **Restore purchase**. Do the same on a new device.
You are not charged again.

### Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase.

### Where does my data go?

**Nowhere.** Judging and history stay on your devices. See the
[privacy policy](privacy-en.html) for details.

### What happens if I delete the app?

Your motions, sounds and history are removed from the device.
